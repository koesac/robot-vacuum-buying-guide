5. Questions that can change the winner

Two floors: one robot or two?

Multi-floor maps do not move a robot between floors. Verify upstairs cleaning without the dock, mop-wash return behaviour, map switching and carrying requirements. Compare keeping the i7 upstairs against replacing it entirely.

Manufacturer multi-floor guidance
Dog hair and real cleaning performance

Find comparable carpet pickup, hair-wrap, bin-emptying and small-object tests. Record brush-cleaning effort. Advertised pressure is not a debris pickup score; we have not collected comparable results for every model.

Allergies: bagless is not automatically better

Check dust released during emptying, bag closure, filter access and evidence of leakage. Bag prices alone do not establish which is preferable. No model receives an allergy rating here without evidence.

Dock footprint and real maintenance space

Measure floor area plus access above tanks and around the station. Use the exact model manual for clearance; do not apply another model’s measurements. Record dirty-water emptying and tray-cleaning effort.

Alexa and Home Assistant are different requirements

Alexa support does not establish named-room commands. Roborock’s official Home Assistant integration uses local communication where supported but still needs cloud access for some functions. Verify the exact model, local network reachability and account setup.

Repairability, batteries and software support

Before checkout, ask for battery price, replacement procedure, genuine brush/filter availability, UK repair route, warranty terms and documented update support. None is automatically inferred from brand reputation.

Wet stations create work too

Xiaomi recommends cleaning the X20+ dirty-water tank after each use. A self-cleaning mop is not a self-maintaining machine. Check replacement schedules rather than assuming every kit is an annual purchase.

Noise, rugs, privacy and seller details

Check auto-empty noise separately from cleaning noise; verify mop clearance/removal on rugs; inspect camera and cloud settings; confirm the UK seller, exact SKU, new condition, returns and warranty route. An Amazon listing alone does not establish any of these.
